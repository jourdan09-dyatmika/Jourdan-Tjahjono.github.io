About Me

The person behind the lens

Who I Am

Constructing Dreams Capturing Emotions
Creating Memories

I'm Jourdan Tjahjono, a photographer, programmer, luthier, 3D printer, sim racer, etc.

My journey began at a young age doing paper crafts before moving on to furniture assembly and repair at 12, moving on to electronics repair, programming, 3d printing, sim racing, and eventually photography.

Every race is a story. Every portrait is a personality. Every commercial is a message. My mission is to capture these stories with authenticity, creativity, and technical excellence.

My Journey

2014

The Beginning

Started photography with my parents phone and my constructions with paper.

2018

First hand at understanding things

Began to invest myself more into technology and learning to code.

2018

Went deep into projects

Began my bigger projects like furniture assembly and repair, electronics repair.

2020

3D renaissance

First began with a cheap 3d printer while learnign to repair, modify, assemble, etc. Went on to start my first 3d printer project and dive into sodering and deeper electronics.

2022

Mechanical dive

First sim racing wheel with a older g29 wheel and dived deeper into motorsport and mechanical/automotive engineering. Investing mroe in understanding complex mechanical systems.

2023

Advancing to a new front

Addvancement into a better 3d printer, beginning to experiment with multimaterial projects.

2024

The culmination of skills

First project that really tested multiple skills from 3d pinting parts to mechanical design and hardware assembly, to electircal engineering and circut design. The first dive into luthier work, the custom 3d printed HHS telestrat.

2025

The move to capturing moments

Moving onto a real camera and leaning the deeper aspects of photography and post processing, while also working on more complex projects.

2026

Today

Creating this poor excuse of a website and diving into my IGCSEs blindly.
